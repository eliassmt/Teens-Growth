Privacy Policy & Safety

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

TEENUP is committed to protecting your privacy:

  • ✅ We don't collect unnecessary personal information
  • ✅ We never sell your data to third parties
  • ✅ All content is age-appropriate and moderated
  • ✅ We follow COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
  • ✅ You can use the platform without creating an account
  • ✅ If you create an account, we protect it with encryption

What We Collect (If You Sign Up)

  • Username (optional)
  • Email address (encrypted)
  • Your learning progress (to personalize recommendations)

What We Don't Collect

  • ❌ Your real name
  • ❌ Phone number
  • ❌ Address or location
  • ❌ Payment information
  • ❌ Browsing history outside TEENUP

Safety Tips for Users

  • Never share personal information in comments
  • Report inappropriate content immediately
  • Use a strong password if you create an account
  • Talk to trusted adults about your online activities
